# Acme Demo Foundation — Procurement Policy [SAMPLE]

*This is sample data for demo purposes. Do not use as a real policy.*

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Approving authority: Demo Board of Directors
Owner: Demo Compliance Office

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every purchase of goods or services by Acme
Demo Foundation valued at $100 USD or more. Below that threshold,
the General Petty Cash Procedure applies.

## 2. Three Quotes Rule

For any purchase $1,000 USD or more, the requisitioner must obtain
three independent written quotes before approval. The quotes must:

- Come from three legally distinct suppliers
- Be dated within 30 days of the requisition
- Itemise the exact same scope of goods/services
- Be signed (digital or wet) by the supplier representative

## 3. Sign-off Thresholds

| Value (USD)    | Required signature                       |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| $100 - $999    | Line manager                             |
| $1,000 - $4,999| Line manager + Finance Officer           |
| $5,000 - $24,999| Line manager + Finance Director         |
| $25,000+       | Line manager + Finance Director + ED    |

## 4. Vendor Registration

Every supplier paid by Acme Demo Foundation must be registered with
their country's business registry (e.g. Companies House, CAC, SEC).
The certificate of registration must be on file before any payment
is processed.

## 5. Invoice Requirements

Every invoice must include:

- Supplier legal name (matching the registration certificate)
- Supplier registered address
- Tax/VAT registration number (where applicable)
- Itemised line items with unit price and quantity
- Total amount in the currency of payment
- Date of issue
- Unique invoice number from the supplier's system
- Bank account details for payment

## 6. Goods Received Note (GRN)

For physical goods, a GRN signed by the receiving officer must be
filed within 5 working days of delivery. The GRN must confirm:

- Items received match the purchase order
- Quantity received
- Condition on arrival
- Any discrepancies

## 7. Travel Advances

Travel advances must be retired within 14 days of return. Retirement
requires original receipts for all expenses claimed.

## 8. Cash Limit

No single cash payment may exceed $500 USD equivalent. All payments
above that threshold must be made by bank transfer.

## 9. Per Diem

Per diem rates follow the published Acme Demo Foundation rate card.
Per diems may not exceed published rates without ED approval.

## 10. Receipts

Original receipts (not photocopies) must accompany every payment
voucher. Where the original is unavailable, a sworn affidavit from
the requisitioner is required.
