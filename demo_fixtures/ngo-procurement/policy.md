## Section A — Procurement

### A1. Three Quotes

For any procurement above $1,000 USD, three independent written
quotes are required. Quotes must be on supplier letterhead, dated
within 30 days, and signed.

### A2. Sole Source Justification

Sole source purchases above $1,000 require written ED approval and a
sole-source memo explaining why competitive quotes weren't sought.

### A3. Vendor Due Diligence

Every vendor must be registered in their country's business registry
before any payment is made. The registration certificate must be on
file in the vendor master record.

### A4. Invoice Completeness

Invoices must show: supplier legal name, registered address, tax
registration, line items with unit price + quantity, total in
payment currency, date of issue, unique invoice number, bank details.

### A5. Goods Received Note

Every physical-goods delivery requires a GRN signed by the receiving
officer within 5 working days. Discrepancies must be noted on the GRN
and reconciled before payment.

### A6. Original Receipts

Payment vouchers must be accompanied by original receipts, not
photocopies. Sworn affidavits are accepted where the original is
genuinely unavailable.

## Section B — Travel

### B1. Travel Advance Retirement

Travel advances must be retired within 14 days of return with
original receipts. Unretired advances are deducted from the next
salary cycle.

### B2. Cash Limit

No single cash payment may exceed $500 USD equivalent. All larger
payments via bank transfer.

### B3. Per Diem

Per diem rates are set in the Acme Demo Foundation rate card. Per
diems above published rates require ED approval.

### B4. Travel Booking

Air travel must be booked through the approved travel agent. Direct
bookings require prior Finance Director approval.

## Section C — Sign-off Authority

| Value (USD)     | Required signatures                           |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Up to $999      | Line manager                                  |
| $1,000 - $4,999 | Line manager + Finance Officer                |
| $5,000 - $24,999| Line manager + Finance Director               |
| $25,000+        | Line manager + Finance Director + ED          |

## Section D — Documentation Retention

All procurement documentation retained for 7 years. Donor-specific
retention requirements (USAID, BMGF, etc.) take precedence where
longer. Audit access on demand.
