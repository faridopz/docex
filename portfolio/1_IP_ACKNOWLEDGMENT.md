**1. Background.**
The Developer has, independently and using his own time, equipment and resources, designed and developed a software product for document intelligence, compliance and finance automation, known as **"DOCex"** — together with its underlying framework, source code, architecture, methods and know-how (collectively, the **"Product IP"**).

**2. Ownership of the Product.**
TAConnect acknowledges and agrees that the Product and all Product IP are the **sole and exclusive property of the Developer**. TAConnect does not, and will not, claim any right, title or interest in the Product IP — including under the *Intellectual Property Rights* clause of the Volunteer Agreement dated 5 January 2026.

**3. TAConnect Build — full-product licence.**
The Developer will make available to TAConnect a **full deployment of the Product, including all modules** (the **"TAConnect Build"**), and grants TAConnect a **non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the full Product for its internal operations** for the agreed period. This licence does **not** transfer ownership of any Product IP.

**4. Source access for the Build.**
To build, deploy and maintain the TAConnect Build, the Developer may give designated TAConnect personnel access to a **copy ("fork") of the Product source code**, solely for the purpose of the TAConnect Build. Such access does **not** transfer ownership of the Product IP. Configuration, customisation and contributions made by TAConnect personnel to that fork **belong to TAConnect and remain within the TAConnect Build** — they are not incorporated into, and create no claim over, the Developer's core Product, which the Developer remains free to develop and license to others.

**5. Disclosure and no conflict.**
The Developer has disclosed the existence of the Product to TAConnect. The parties confirm that the Developer's development of the Product, conducted on his own time and resources, is disclosed under the *Conflict of Interest* clause of the Volunteer Agreement and is not in conflict with his volunteer duties (which relate to Monitoring & Evaluation).

**6. General.**
This acknowledgment records the parties' understanding regarding ownership and is intended to be legally binding once signed. It is subject to review by each party's legal advisers.
