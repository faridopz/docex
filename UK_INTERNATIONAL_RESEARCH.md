# UK & International NGO Landscape (Quick Research)

**Your friend in England is a good entry point. Here's the landscape.**

---

## UK NGO Market (Where Your Friend Can Help)

### Size & Opportunity

- **~170,000 registered charities in the UK** (Charity Commission)
- **~40,000 are "larger charities"** (budget >₦200m/year)
- **~5,000 are "major charities"** (budget >₦2b/year) — your target

### Major UK Charities (Audit-Heavy, Multi-Grant)

| Charity | Revenue/Year | Why They Need This |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Oxfam** | £400m | International operations, multiple donors, audit-intensive |
| **Save the Children UK** | £1b+ | Global programs, USAID + EU compliance, complex workflows |
| **British Red Cross** | £400m | Multiple geographies, donor compliance |
| **Cancer Research UK** | £700m+ | Large grants, research funding, compliance heavy |
| **Wellcome Trust** | £1.1b (grant-maker) | Gives grants to organizations, needs grantee oversight |
| **NICE (National Institute for Health)** | £200m+ | Government funding, audit trail required |

**These orgs have:**
- 50–1000+ staff
- Multiple departments
- International operations
- Strict audit requirements (Charity Commission + donors)
- Budget: can afford ₦500k–₦2m/month for systems

---

### Smaller UK Charities (Good Starters)

- **International Development orgs:** Christian Aid, Tearfund, Concern, Action Against Hunger
- **Humanitarian orgs:** Médecins Sans Frontières UK, DEC (Disaster Emergency Committee)
- **Global Health:** Médecins du Monde, Global Fund partnerships

**Why start here:**
- Smaller = faster decision (CFO can say yes without board approval)
- Same problem: complex grants, audit stress
- Budget: ₦250k–₦500k/month (profitable for you)

## International Markets (Beyond UK)

### East Africa (Closest to You)

**Kenya:**
- ~20,000 registered NGOs
- High audit requirements (similar to Nigeria)
- Donors: USAID, World Bank, bilateral
- Entry: Warm intros through NEEM's partners

**Uganda, Tanzania, Ethiopia:**
- Similar dynamics
- Growing NGO sector
- Less mature tech adoption (good for early movers)

**Pricing:** ₦300k–₦400k/month (lower than UK, higher than Nigeria because these are international orgs)

---

### South Africa

- Most developed NGO sector in Africa
- High audit standards
- Many international NGO HQs for Southern Africa
- Pricing: ₦400k–₦600k/month

---

### South Asia (India, Bangladesh, Pakistan)

- Massive NGO sector (100k+ in India alone)
- High compliance requirements (donor driven)
- Growing tech adoption
- Pricing: ₦200k–₦400k/month (lower because competition is higher)

**Entry:** Harder (you need local connections). Skip Year 1.

---

### Global (International NGOs with Multi-Country Operations)

- **USAID grantees** (operating in 50+ countries)
- **Save the Children International** (operates in 120 countries)
- **Mercy Corps** (operates in 40+ countries)
- **CARE International** (operates in 100+ countries)

**Why they're valuable:**
- One deal = ₦500k–₦2m/month (large operations)
- They use the system in ALL their country offices
- Repeat revenue (scales with them)

**Entry:** LinkedIn to their Global Finance Director. "I built a system for a grantee in Nigeria. We can help you standardize finance workflows across all your country offices."

**Pricing:** ₦1m–₦5m+/month (they can afford it)

## Pricing Across Markets

| Market | Org Size | Budget/Month | Your Price | Margin |
|--------|----------|-------------|-----------|--------|
| Nigeria (Local NGO) | 50–100 staff | ₦300–600k | ₦400k | 42% |
| UK Small Charity | 100–500 staff | £5k–15k | £12k (₦6.6m) | 50%+ |
| UK Major Charity | 1000+ staff | £50k–100k | £30k (₦16.5m) | 60%+ |
| East Africa | 100–500 staff | ₦400–800k | ₦500k | 45% |
| International NGO (Multi-country) | 5000+ staff | $50k–200k | $100k (₦55m) | 70%+ |

**Key insight:** As you move up-market (bigger orgs), your margins improve dramatically. UK charities can support 2–3x your asking price.
