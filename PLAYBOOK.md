# DOCex Playbook — 3 Paying Clients by End of July

**Where we are:** May 24, 2026. Product works. TA Connect warm. Zero spend on infra so far. Solo founder, 1-2 hours/day.

**The goal:** 3 signed paying contracts ($3K-$5K+/year each) by July 31. That's roughly $10K-$15K in committed annual revenue — the foundation for the $10K MRR ambition.

---

## The honest math

60 days. 1.5 hours/day average ≈ 90 total hours. Three enterprise contracts in 90 hours from a cold start is borderline impossible **unless two things are true:**

1. **TA Connect closes paid within the next 4 weeks.** Not "pilot." Not "interest." Money committed. They are your highest-probability deal.
2. **TA Connect refers two more warm intros.** Cold outreach at 1-2 hrs/day will not produce 2 more paid contracts in 60 days. Warm intros will.

If both happen, July 31 is real. If neither has happened by mid-June, the honest revised target becomes 1 paid + 2 paid pilots (the option you ruled out — but don't lie to yourself when the data says it).

Check this every Friday.

---

## Daily ritual (1-2 hours, every weekday)

**Pick one anchor. Do it before lunch. Then stop.**

**30 min — One conversation action** *(the only non-negotiable)*

Email a warm contact. Reply to a thread. DM on LinkedIn. Confirm a meeting. Send a recap. Send a follow-up. Whatever moves one relationship forward by one step.

**30 min — One sharpen task**

Polish a slide. Write a paragraph of the pilot proposal. Refine the demo script. Update the warm list. Read 5 minutes of a competitor's site. Improve one rulebook.

**15 min — One input**

Podcast on the way somewhere. Skim Devex newsletter. Read one NGO compliance article. Look up one Abuja event. Note what you learned in a one-line journal.

That's 1 hour 15 min hard floor, 2 hours hard ceiling. **Doing this every weekday compounds. Doing 4 hours one day and zero for three days does not.**

---

## Weekly ritual

**Monday (15 min):** What is the ONE outcome that matters this week? Write it on a sticky note. Examples: *"TA Connect demo scheduled." "20-name warm list done." "Pilot proposal v1 sent."*

**Friday (30 min):** What moved? What didn't? What's one thing you'd do differently next week? Update the warm list. Send any follow-ups you owe.

**Saturday + Sunday:** Rest. Family. If you have 20 minutes, send a single follow-up email. Otherwise — full stop.

## Free stack (no money, first 3 clients)

You don't need to spend a kobo on infra until you have paying customers. Here's what runs free until the first contract pays for everything:

- **Demo:** Run from your laptop. Google Meet screen-share. If you need a public URL, ngrok free tier (1 tunnel, fine for demos).
- **CRM:** Notion or one Google Sheet. Columns: *Org, Contact, Stage, Last touch, Next action, Notes.*
- **Email:** Gmail. Use Gmail SMTP for DOCex notifications (free).
- **Domain:** Defer 2 weeks. When ready: `docex.africa` or `docex.ng` on Namecheap (~$10/year).
- **Calendar:** Google Calendar + free Calendly when you start booking demos.
- **Pilot agreement template:** One Google Doc, save as template, copy + customize per client.
- **When to spend:** First $3K-$5K contract → Vercel ($20/mo) + Railway ($5-20/mo) + domain. Total ~$30/mo. The contract pays for 4-6 years of infra.

## If you fall off

You will. Ramadan recovery + Eid + family + life happens. Two rules:

1. **Never miss two days in a row.** One bad day is recovery. Two is a habit forming.
2. **Floor day = 15 minutes.** On the worst day, send one email. That's the floor. Closing the gap to zero is what kills businesses, not slow weeks.

No shame. Just open this file Monday morning and pick up.
