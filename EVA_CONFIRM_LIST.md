## 1. Confirmed from your Procurement Policy

We built your approval chain from these:

| Amount | Who approves | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Below ₦300,000** | Departmental heads or delegates (not below Coordinator grade) | *Thresholds and Approval* |
| **₦300,000 – ₦10,000,000** | **Executive Director** | *"All procurements involving N300,000 or more … must be approved by the executive director"* |
| **₦10,000,000 and above** | **Board Chair + at least one Finance Committee member** | *"…must be approved by the board chair and at least a member of the finance committee of the board"* |

Also read:

- **Three quotations** required at **₦300,000 or more**. If fewer than three are
  received, the reason must be documented.
- **Single-source procurement** at **₦200,000 or more** requires a written
  justification (procurement waiver).

**In the system this means:** a ₦299,999 payment stops at your Finance Team
Lead. At ₦300,000 it routes to the ED. At ₦10,000,000 it routes to the Board —
and nobody can approve out of order or skip a step.

> **Please confirm these three bands are current.** They are the backbone of
> everything else.

## 3. Not stated anywhere — we need your answer

We searched all eighteen documents. These are genuinely absent, not missed:

### a. Advance retirement window ⚠️

**No timeframe appears in POL-FIN-300.01 (Activity Advances) or POL-FIN-300.04
(Travel Advance).** Neither document states how many days staff have to retire
an advance.

- What is the practice today?
- Do you want the system to enforce it — flag overdue advances, and block a
  second advance while a first is unretired?

*This is the most valuable gap on the list. An advance-aging report makes an
invisible problem visible, and it is usually the first thing an auditor asks
about.*

### b. Advance limits

No maximum advance amount is stated in POL-FIN-300.01 to .04. Is there a
ceiling, and does it differ by advance type (activity / travel / salary /
vendor)?

### c. Petty cash ceiling

POL-FIN-100.02 mentions ₦40,000 — but only inside a worked example
(*"It is assumed that EVA/FCT establishes a Petty Cash Fund…"*). **We have not
treated an illustration as a policy limit.** What is the real ceiling, and does
it differ per office?

### d. Statutory rates

PAYE bands, pension rate, WHT rates by payment type, VAT treatment. Needed
before we can compute deductions automatically.

### e. Grant codes and agreement periods

Donor, project code, start date, end date for each active agreement. This drives
the check that blocks a cost being charged outside its agreement period — one of
the most common audit findings.

### f. "Refinancing" — the one we will not guess ⚠️

Our working interpretation: **cost recovery**, where salary cost is charged back
to a donor grant and shown as a negative because it offsets the expense line.

**Is that right?** We are not writing any payroll code until you confirm,
because a wrong sign convention in a payroll system is a serious defect and is
not something we can infer safely.

### g. Multi-office

Are HQ / State / field offices a reporting dimension on every transaction, or
would you prefer separate instances?

### h. QuickBooks export

Which format, and does it import as journals or as bills?
