# DOCex — Soul

## Why This Exists
Somewhere in Abuja right now a sub-award officer at an NGO
is sitting with 200 application documents open in tabs.
She has 3 days to shortlist 10 partners from 200 submissions.
She will miss good organisations because she ran out of time.
She will approve weaker ones because their document looked
polished. None of that reflects the actual capacity of the
partners on the ground.

DOCex exists so that never has to happen again.

## What We Believe
- The best local partners get overlooked because the
  application process favours those who write well,
  not those who can deliver
- AI should reduce that bias, not increase it
- Tools built for Africa should be built with Africa in mind
- Simple beats clever every time
- The person uploading documents at 11pm before a deadline
  deserves software that respects their time
- A tool is only valuable if the person who needs it
  can actually use it without training

## How This Should Feel To Use
Fast. Trustworthy. Like it was built by someone who has
actually sat in an NGO office and felt the pain.
Not corporate. Not generic. Human.

When someone uploads 50 documents and hits screen,
they should feel relief — not anxiety.
The results should feel like a trusted colleague
reviewed everything overnight and left clear notes.

## What Success Looks Like
A grants officer in Abuja screens 150 applications
in 2 hours instead of 2 weeks.
She finds 3 strong partners she would have missed.
One of them delivers a program that reaches 10,000 people.

That is what this software is for.

## Copy and Tone
Every word in this product should feel like it was
written for someone working late on a deadline.

Good copy examples:
- "Train your AI" not "Configure settings"
- "Start screening" not "Submit"
- "See full breakdown" not "View details"
- "Your results are ready" not "Process complete"
- "Looks like you have not screened anything yet —
   upload your first batch to get started"
  not "No data found"

Never write like enterprise software.
Never write like a startup trying to sound smart.
Write like a helpful colleague who knows the work.

## For The Agent Reading This
When you build a feature ask:
Does this make the grants officer's life simpler
or more complicated?

If it is more complicated, we do not build it.
When in doubt, do less better.

The person using this tool is not a developer.
She is smart, overworked, and trying to do good work
in a difficult environment. Every decision should
respect that.
